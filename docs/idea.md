# RETRO BINGO — Minimum Viable Product

## OBJECTIVE

RETRO BINGO is a lightweight, ambient game played during an agile retrospective.

Players listen for recurring retrospective themes.  
When themes are mentioned, they mark them on their card.  

To WIN, a player must achieve a BINGO.

The game continues even after the first WIN.

---

# COMPONENTS

- 1 QR Code (displayed at the start of the RETRO)
- 1 Game Session
- A shared pool of RETRO themes
- 1 unique 5×5 BINGO CARD per player
- 1 FREE tile (center square)

---

# SETUP

1. The facilitator displays the QR Code.
2. Players scan the QR Code.
3. Players enter their name.
4. Each player receives a randomly generated BINGO CARD.
5. The center tile is marked as FREE.

All cards are generated from the same theme pool.  
Each card layout is unique.

---

# THE BINGO CARD

- Grid size: 5 × 5
- Center tile: FREE (automatically marked)
- Remaining tiles: RETRO themes
- Tiles can be marked and unmarked by tapping/clicking

---

# HOW TO PLAY

1. The RETRO begins.
2. As themes are mentioned in discussion, players mark matching tiles.
3. Marking is individual. There is no host-controlled calling.
4. Play continues naturally alongside the RETRO conversation.

The game must not interrupt or dominate the session.

---

# WIN CONDITIONS

## BINGO (WIN CONDITION)

A player achieves BINGO when they complete:

- Any horizontal row  
- Any vertical column  
- Any diagonal  

When a player achieves BINGO:

- They receive a clear BINGO notification.
- All players receive a notification announcing the BINGO.
- That player cannot achieve BINGO again.

The game continues after a BINGO.

Other players may also achieve BINGO.

To WIN is to achieve BINGO.

---

## FULL HOUSE (SECONDARY OBJECTIVE)

After achieving BINGO, a player may continue marking tiles.

A FULL HOUSE is achieved when all 25 tiles (including FREE) are marked.

When a player achieves FULL HOUSE:

- They receive a distinct FULL HOUSE notification.
- All players receive a special FULL HOUSE announcement.

FULL HOUSE does not end the game.

---

# GAME FLOW

1. Players join.
2. RETRO discussion proceeds.
3. Players mark tiles as themes arise.
4. One or more players achieve BINGO.
5. Players may continue toward FULL HOUSE.
6. The RETRO concludes naturally.

---

# DESIGN PRINCIPLES

- Zero friction to join
- No facilitator overhead
- Mobile-first interaction
- Minimal visual noise
- Celebrations are brief and clear
- Interface and design must be as MINIMAL and SIMPLE as possible to achieve the MVP

RETRO BINGO should support the RETRO — not compete with it.
